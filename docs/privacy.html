Sum of Parts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

"Sum of Parts publishing" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Sum of Parts channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information about viewers or anyone else.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the channel owner's own channel, to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and publish times, and to read the channel's name to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Graph API): the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, to publish and schedule the channel's videos and post a comment with the link to the full video.

What is stored

Only the access tokens that let the tool post to the owner's accounts. They are stored on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in the owner's GitHub repository, and are used for nothing else. The tool stores no personal data about any other person, does not use cookies, and does not show ads.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram, where the owner chose to publish them.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and from Facebook's Business Integrations settings. Deleting the tokens stops all access immediately.

Third-party terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.
