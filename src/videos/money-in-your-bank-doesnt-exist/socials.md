## Before posting anywhere

- **Music licence:** the clips use "Pizzicato Polka" from Envato Elements. Envato licences are registered per project. When you register, cover these clips and these platforms (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook), or register each clip. That way you can answer any claim with the licence certificate.
- **Captions are burned in:** turn **off** each platform's auto-captions so the words don't show twice.

## Instagram Reels

Settings for every Reel:
- **Cover:** upload the custom cover from `out/shorts/covers/` (Edit cover > Add from camera roll). Covers are 1080x1920 with the title and object inside the middle 1080x1440, so the 3:4 profile grid shows them whole: no crop adjusting needed.
- **Auto captions:** off (they're burned in).
- **Alt text** (Advanced settings > Accessibility): see each clip below.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5, at the end of the caption.
- Link: Instagram captions aren't clickable. Say "full video on YouTube, link in bio", and keep the YouTube channel link in your bio.

### Reel 1: hook
Caption:
```
Your banking app says $4,280. So where is that money? 🏦

Not in a vault with your name on it. It's a promise. And the textbook story, that banks lend out your savings, isn't how it works: the Bank of England says lending creates deposits.

Source: Bank of England, 2014.
Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#money #banking #personalfinance #economics #finance
```

Then a textbook page about the money multiplier is ripped in half, revealing a Bank of England quote: "the act of lending creates deposits".`

### Reel 2: typed money
Caption:
```
Banks don't lend out your savings. They type new money. ⌨️

When a bank approves a loan, it makes two entries: you owe the bank, and your account goes up. That money didn't exist a second ago. And when the loan is repaid, the money disappears again.

Source: Bank of England, 2014. Sarah's car loan is an example.
Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#money #banking #economics #finance #moneyfacts
```
Alt text: `Paper-cut animation: a bank ledger with "Bank owns" and "Bank owes" pages; a typewriter types "Loan to Sarah: $20,000" and "Sarah's deposit: $20,000", then a "New money" banner unrolls. Payment slips go into a shredder until the banner reads +$0, then a paper town where new loans pop up and repayments fold away.`

### Reel 3: never printed
Caption:
```
9 in 10 dollars have never been printed. 💵

US money (M2) is about $23.2 trillion. Physical cash is about $2.5 trillion of it. The rest is just numbers in bank accounts: your balance is an IOU from your bank. And since March 2020, US banks are required to keep 0% in reserve.

Sources: Federal Reserve H.6 (July 2026), Bank of England (2014), Regulation D (2020).
Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#money #economics #banking #finance #moneyfacts
```
Alt text: `Paper-cut animation: a grid of 100 squares; 11 turn into banknotes and the rest turn amber, with the label "9 in 10 never printed", then a smaller UK grid labelled 97%. A balance card flips over to read "IOU", and an empty box labelled "Required reserves" sits next to a big "0%".`
