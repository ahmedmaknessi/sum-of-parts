## Instagram Reels

No custom covers for video 01 (flat style): the Reel uses a frame of the video. Captions are burned in, so auto-captions stay off. Alt text is added by hand after posting.

### Reel 1: the hook
Caption:
```
$100 a month for 40 years. How much do you end up with? 💰

Most people guess around $50,000. The real answer: $262,481. That's $100 a month invested at 7% a year (the long-run S&P 500 average after inflation), from 25 to 65.

Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#compoundinterest #investing #personalfinance #money #moneyfacts
```
Alt text: `Animation on a dark navy background: "$100" appears small while a month counter ticks quickly, then a question mark, then "$262,481" lands in large type with an amber underline.`

### Reel 2: year 11
Caption:
```
There's a year when your money starts out-earning you. 📈

Invest $100 a month at 7% a year. In year 11, your investments earn $1,290 on their own: more than the $1,200 you put in that year. From then on, the gap only grows.

Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#compoundinterest #investing #money #personalfinance #finance
```
Alt text: `Animation: two bars per year scroll by, a cream bar for the $1,200 you deposit and an amber bar for the growth earned. At year 11 the amber bar passes the cream one and a label reads "Year 11: your money out-earns you".`

### Reel 3: the last decade
Caption:
```
Why the last 10 years matter most. ⏳

$100 a month at 7% for 40 years. Decade by decade the account grows by $17K, $35K, $70K, then $140K. The last 10 years alone add more than the first 30 combined. Same $100 a month the whole time: the only thing that changed was time.

Full video on YouTube (link in bio). Education, not financial advice.

#compoundinterest #investing #personalfinance #money #longterminvesting
```
Alt text: `Animation: four vertical bars appear one by one, $17K, $35K, $70K and an amber $140K. The first three bars stack on top of each other next to the fourth and are still shorter. Label: "Last 10 years > first 30 years".`
